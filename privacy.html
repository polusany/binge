Privacy on Binge.

Visitor activity

Binge records your network IP address, the page requested and the visit time for site administration and abuse prevention. Only BINGERET can view or delete these records. Records are removed after 30 days when the site is accessed; the log is also capped at the latest 10,000 visits.

Blocked visitors

If the administrator blocks an IP address to prevent future visits, the address and block time remain in a private block list until the administrator unblocks it. Deleting ordinary visit records does not remove an active block. A shared IP address can affect multiple people.

Cookies

An essential session cookie is used when the administrator signs in. It expires after eight hours. Ordinary visitors do not need an account or a login cookie to watch, read or download published content.

Downloads and hosting

Downloads are saved by your browser to the location configured on your device. The hosting provider may also process technical connection logs according to its own privacy practices. This site does not add advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

Requests

For questions about content or visitor records, contact the site operator through the channel where this website was shared with you.
